Privacy policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

The short version

  • I only collect what you choose to give me: your email when you join the book waitlist, and anything you send me by email. My email newsletter runs on Substack, which handles those subscriptions.
  • I use it to send you the emails you signed up for and to reply to you. Nothing else.
  • I never sell or rent your information.
  • You can unsubscribe or ask me to delete your information at any time by emailing keeganpurnell@gmail.com.

1. Who I am

This website is run by Keegan Purnell, an author and marketer based in Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada. I am responsible for the personal information collected through it. You can reach me at keeganpurnell@gmail.com.

2. What I collect

  • Email newsletter: when you subscribe on Substack, Substack collects your email address and shares it with me so I can send you my essays. Substack’s own privacy policy also applies.
  • Book waitlist: your email address, when you join the waitlist for Enjoy Entrepreneurship.
  • Messages: anything you send me by email, including your name and email address.

This site has no accounts, no comments and no payments. Book purchases happen on Amazon or other retailers, under their own privacy policies.

3. How I use it

  • To send the emails you asked for: new articles, book news and updates on what I’m building, or a launch announcement for the next book.
  • To reply to your messages, including bulk order, workshop, speaking and press requests.

I will not use your information for any other purpose without asking you first.

4. Your consent and unsubscribing

By joining the waitlist or subscribing on Substack, you agree to receive emails from me. Every email identifies me and tells you how to unsubscribe. Substack emails have an unsubscribe link at the bottom. For anything else, reply “unsubscribe” or write to keeganpurnell@gmail.com, and I will remove you within 10 business days, as Canada’s Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL) requires.

5. Who else handles your information

I don’t sell, rent or trade your information. A small number of service providers help run this site and may process it on my behalf:

  • Substack runs my email newsletter: it stores subscriber emails and sends my essays. Substack is based in the United States.
  • Netlify hosts this website and stores book waitlist sign-ups. Netlify is based in the United States, so your information may be stored and processed there.
  • Google provides the email service I use to send messages and receive replies. This site’s typefaces are served from the site itself, so loading a page does not contact Google.

If I change newsletter services in the future, I will update this policy to name the new one.

6. Cookies and tracking

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If I add privacy-friendly visitor statistics in the future, I will update this page first. Links to other websites, such as Amazon, LinkedIn and Medium, take you to sites with their own privacy policies.

7. How long I keep it

I keep your name and email for as long as you stay subscribed. When you unsubscribe, I delete your details from my list. I keep emails you send me only as long as needed to respond and follow up.

8. Your rights

Under Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), you can ask to see the personal information I hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted. Email me and I’ll respond within 30 days. If you have a concern I can’t resolve, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

9. Keeping it safe

I use reputable providers with secure, encrypted connections, and only I have access to sign-up information.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, I’ll update it here and change the date at the top. If a change affects how I use information you’ve already given me, I’ll ask for your consent again.

This policy is written in plain language to explain how the site handles your information. It is not legal advice.
